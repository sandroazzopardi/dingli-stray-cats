JOIN US

Caring for stray and feral cats is a community effort. There are many ways you can support our cats. Even a small amount of your time can help us keep our colonies fed, cared for and monitored.

FEEDING & COLONY CARE

Help with feeding, checking colonies and keeping feeding areas clean and tidy.

TRAP-NEUTER-RETURN (TNR)

Assist with trapping, transporting cats and returning them safely after veterinary treatment.

BCRS & FOOD COLLECTIONS

Help collect donated bottles, cans and cat food that support our colonies.
