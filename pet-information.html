TNR | Trap - Neuter - Return

A humane and practical approach to managing stray and feral cat colonies, while helping to reduce the number of kittens born into an uncertain future.

When carried out responsibly and as an ongoing process, TNR can help create healthier and more stable cat colonies over time.

Cat Microchipping

In Malta, microchipping for pet cats kept at home is currently voluntary, though new EU-wide traceability and welfare rules are set to phase in mandatory requirements.

Travel and Import: If you travel internationally with your cat or bring a cat across borders into Malta, a microchip (ISO-compliant 11784/11785) is strictly mandatory under EU pet travel regulations.
