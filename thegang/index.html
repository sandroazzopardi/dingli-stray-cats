Meet Pumpkinhead🧡 The face of the Dingli cat mafia. 😼 Big ginger tom. Grumpy every single day. Senior citizen. And not neutered — yet. When Pumpkinhead strolls down the creek, every other cat lowers its eyes, every bowl is emptied, and even the rats pretend not to see him. He is a big ginger tomcat with a face that only ever has one expression — grumpy. But don't let the frown fool you — he's old, tired, and just wants to eat, sleep, and judge you from a sunny rubble wall. Rough years on these streets. He deserves a soft retirement. ♥️
