BCRS Bottle Donations · Malta

Your empty bottles can make a difference for Dingli's stray cats.

Don't throw away the opportunity to help. Donate your eligible BCRS bottles and cans, and the proceeds will help us provide food and essential care for the stray cats we look after in Dingli.

Arrange a Bottle Donation

Small bottles. Meaningful support.

At Dingli Stray Cats, we spend our free time feeding and caring for local stray and feral cats and supporting Trap-Neuter-Return (TNR) efforts. Donating BCRS containers is a simple way to help us keep going without making a monetary donation.

Whether you have a few bottles at home or regularly collect them at work, your contribution is appreciated.

How to donate your BCRS bottles

STEP 01

Save your containers

Set aside empty bottles and cans eligible for Malta's BCRS deposit-return scheme.

STEP 02

Get in touch

Tell us approximately how many containers you have and where you are located.

STEP 03

Arrange a collection

We'll coordinate a suitable pick-up where possible, depending on volunteer availability.

Which bottles and cans can I donate?

We welcome empty containers that are eligible for the BCRS scheme in Malta. Please keep the containers in a condition suitable for return, with readable barcodes where applicable. Eligibility and return requirements are set by BCRS, so check the official BCRS website if you're unsure.

Please contact us before leaving any bottles at a location. We do not operate an unattended public drop-off point.

Could your workplace or family collect for us?

A small collection box at home, in an office or at a local business can add up over time. If you'd like to collect BCRS bottles and cans on behalf of Dingli Stray Cats, please get in touch. We'd love to hear from you.
